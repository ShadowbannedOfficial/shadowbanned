How to swap pictures

1. Open the folder named images.
2. Replace a file with your photo, using the exact same name.
   - logo.png sits one level up, next to index.html. That is the header logo.
   - images/hero.jpg is the big photo on the home page.
   - images/tee.jpg is the tee.
   - images/hoodie.jpg is the hoodie.
   - images/cap.jpg is the cap.
3. The new file must use the same name, including .jpg or .png.
   If your photo is called IMG_2044.png, rename it to tee.jpg before you drop it in.
   On a Mac, the name is at the top of the file window. On Windows, turn on file extensions so you do not end up with tee.jpg.jpg.
4. Save, then refresh the browser. If the old picture stays, hold Shift and click reload.

jpg or png both work if the name matches what the page asks for. Square or portrait photos look best. Very wide phone screenshots will get cropped.

To change words or prices, open shop.html or index.html in TextEdit or Notepad, edit the text between the tags, and save. Do not rename index.html.
